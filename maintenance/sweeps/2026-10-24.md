# Sweep 2026-10-24

This is the full monthly sweep, run the day after the single-source GC AI pass (`2026-10-23.md`), with open network access. Three parallel passes covered official sources, GitHub, and discussion sites, blogs and evals. Every number adopted into the skill was spot-checked against its source page or README.

## Official changes

- **`jev-latest` → `jev-1.14.0`.**
  - TypeSafe gave no date and no notice. The docs showed it by 10-23. vicfei saw the alias resolving to `jev-1.14-20261014` on 10-19, while two other reports still saw 1.13 around 10-20.
  - `jev-preview` points to the same model, and there's no newer version.
  - Unpinned users who tuned thresholds on 1.13 are already on 1.14. The factsheet now says to pin `jev-1.13.0` to restore the old behavior.
- **Images:**
  - The minimum side is now **280 px** (it was 256).
  - New: a 32 MiB budget for all processed images, 1,024-byte reference keys (65,536 bytes in total), and advice to re-save progressive JPEGs as baseline.
  - Still experimental. Still no image-token estimator.
- **Rate limits:** set per organization, in the developer console. The docs no longer give figures, so the 80 req/s and 100K tok/s line was removed.
- **Jaggedness:** still no 1.14 page; the 1.13 page is unchanged. The factsheet's list was realigned with the official nine items: it had dropped Indirection and Date/time and added two items that aren't on the list.
- **Blog:** no posts after 10-14, and no deprecation policy yet. One vendor case study (Jack & Jill, 10-07) is report only.
- **TypeSafe's official skill v0.6.2 (10-23)**, folded into the rules and the factsheet:
  - On 1.14, a companion "does anything fit?" Noul usually beats a `none` option.
  - 0.7 and 0.3 are good starting thresholds for a Noul.
  - Confidence is rarely the gate.
  - Put each item into its own question rather than an ID in a shared list.
  - Positional option names bias the answer.
  - A Score's position gets pulled toward the middle.
- **SDKs:**
  - Python 0.7.5 and JS 0.7.0: unchanged.
  - `@ai-sdk/typesafe-ai` 3.0.26: `box` question (3.0.21) and `insertReference`.
  - AI SDK 7: `experimental_decide` (since `ai` 7.0.128); `experimental_evaluate` is a deprecated alias.
- **Vercel AI Gateway:**
  - `typesafe-ai/jev-1.13.0` and `jev-1.14.0` are listed with image input, 32k context and zero data retention.
  - The unversioned `typesafe-ai/jev` is text-only and is also served by DigitalOcean, which has no ZDR.
  - Per-request ZDR (`zeroDataRetention: true, only: ['typesafe-ai']`) is verified in Vercel's docs and needs a Pro or Enterprise plan. This resolves the unchecked note from the GC AI pass.
- **OpenRouter:**
  - Every Jev model lists 262k context and image input.
  - Its guide says requests over about 32k bytes of text, and every image request, run in long-context mode.
  - The SDK base URL is `https://openrouter.ai/api`.
  - Field report: its System One endpoint silently drops images.
- **New routes:**
  - DigitalOcean Gradient (`typesafe-jev-1.13.0`, text only).
  - Pydantic AI's native `TypeSafeModel`.
  - TypeSafe's own n8n node 0.10.0 added images.

## Rules changed

- **New rule of thumb, promoted from a hunch: "Read Noul probabilities as odds; don't read Choice or Score probabilities that way."** Brussle's 500-per-type study, our tests and earlier reports agree. Noul caveats are kept: it degrades on hard items, and one earlier test found Nouls under-confident.
- **Ask narrow questions:**
  - PremiseCheck added (49.9 → 63.1).
  - Alinia's counter-result added: splitting one policy into five factors lost 6 points, and a two-line gist matched the full policy. The rule now says to split when the judgment really is several facts.
- **Request shape:**
  - `records[17]` fell from 94% to 34% at 100 items.
  - The same question over every line scored them all 0.44 to 0.46 until the question named the line.
  - Packing 32 replies per call shifted scores.
  - Three phrasings blocked 11%, 54% and 79%.
- **Thresholds:** refit when the model changes. 1.14 is stricter in a second report (Parallix: 12 of 43 vs. 24), and `jev-latest` moved without notice.
- **Trust the extremes:** cites TypeSafe's 0.7/0.3 starting thresholds and "confidence is rarely the gate".
- **Options:** descriptive names, not positional ones.
- **Hunch "relative beats absolute":** Score drift (10% of leads changed level) and a missed plausibility score (0.53) added, plus a counter-result (exact lead level 71% vs. 46% for OpenAI Decisions). It stays a hunch.

## Interesting but unproven (report only)

- **Probe bisection over images** (Empatixx/pdfjev): find an answer in an image-only PDF by page and crop Nouls, then highlight it by moving a line until a yes/no flips. About 10 s and $0.013 on a synthetic 200-page PDF. It's a new shape, and the first working form of "text as an image for spans". Watch for a real test.
- **Contact sheets** (DeepAleph/jevreel): 60 labelled frames per 1,568 px sheet, so a minute of video costs one image. About $0.02–0.03 per hour of video. Demo only.
- **Slow Jev plus a fast local tracker:** jev-box-tracking (a Kalman filter that rewinds for late answers) and a self-driving demo (Jev on 1 frame in 4; $1.02 per minute). No accuracy figures.
- **Channel-agent wake and rewind** (Runtype): a Noul wakes the agent, and per-message Nouls assemble its scrollback. The numbers are illustrative.
- **yolo-jev:** 17/19 with 0/15 false alarms vs. YOLO at 0–4/19. Small.
- **Image heat map by grid Choice** (photo-ferret): 1.9-point error on surface coverage across 12 photos.
- **Leaderboards:**
  - AA Decision Index: Jev 1.13 at 58, top decision model; Haiku 5.5 at 61.
  - AIM System One Index: Jev 1.14 about 7th of 59.
  - Opper JevMan: 1.14 is #1.
- **Adoption:** Vercel says 35% of paid teams use it (TechCrunch); OpenRouter's top apps.
- **From the beri.net digest, originals not reached:**
  - Phishing: 62.6% with one question vs. 95.0% with five narrow questions plus a regression.
  - A pre-registered study: wrong criteria scored 16.7%, and 0 of 30 out-of-scope messages were flagged.
  - Langfuse: 91.5% agreement with Claude.
