## Rules changed

- **Window first:** the harm comes from irrelevant or misleading text, not length. A relevant 12k-token document matched excerpts, and full context fixed a two-hop miss; boilerplate caused false pages.
- **Code first:** added "put code-computed facts in the state" (pager 0.63 → 0.92; Breakout 17 → 29).
- **New rule of thumb, "Ask narrow, factual questions, not holistic ones":** promoted because independent reports agree. Ours: vague per-passage questions were at chance, and "is anything missing?" scored high on everything. GC AI: AUC 0.54 → 0.72, "may"/"must", eval-judge holistic 83%. The exception is a clean single category (88% vs. 82% for doc status). Hand-write the questions.
- **Thresholds:** cutoffs ranged from 0.25 to 0.95; per-pattern thresholds; asymmetric bands.
- **Request shape:** packing 72% / keyed 88% / fan-out 96%; SMS spam 62% → 100%; `cite_4` vs. `citations[4]`; pick-from-list 83% at 5 per call vs. 60% at 40.
- **Mutually exclusive options:** near-synonyms wrong at 1.00; spelling out the rule took CUAD from 65% to 90%; describe every option; rewrite people-facing guidance (41 of 80 as written); a question and its negation don't sum to 1.
- **Hunch "relative beats absolute":** supporting evidence added (absolute Scores bunch in the middle). It stays a hunch.
- **Hunch "calibration":** added that Jev's confidence routed well where gpt-5-mini's self-reported confidence didn't (its errors were all at ≥ 0.85).
- **Not a Jev job:** added world knowledge and trivia, security judgments (28 to 40%), and two-hop cross-references, plus the weekday, counting and rhyme numbers.

## Reported failures / limits

These are added to the `capabilities.md` field reports:
- Holistic calls: AUC 0.54; "sound advice" 83%.
- Absolute Scores: half a level harsh, 47 to 63% agreement.
- Subtle-pattern recall: comment remover recall 0.12; some comment-guideline rules had 0% recall.
- Two-hop cross-references: wrong at 0.86.
- Trivia: 57% vs. 93% for Claude.
- Near-synonym labels: wrong at 1.00.
- File and tool criteria: 59% vs. 96%.
- Security judgments: 28 to 40%.
- Injection: an in-question guard line failed; planted text flipped 2 of 5 answers (caught by the fallback).
- Timeouts: a 1.5 s timeout aborted inside the SDK's retry wait.
- One call over all files pulled their scores together (CI checker).
- Choice ties failed calls in the preference-memory project, so it switched to Nouls.

## Interesting but unproven (report only)

- **Agent-loop "signals" registry** (design only): inject Jev results into the agent loop without breaking prompt caching (append, never mutate; inject only on change).
- **Playbook classifier:** 80 verdicts in 17 s; 55 of 80 agreed with the LLM, but the LLM only agreed with itself 73 to 79% of the time. That's too noisy to rank.
- **Codenames:** 415 clues fanned out in 7 s for $0.05 (a toy).
- **Entity resolution POC:** no numbers yet.
- **AI SDK 7 `experimental_evaluate`** and the Vercel gateway `zeroDataRetention` provider option, as used in GC AI's skill. Noted in the factsheet as GC AI's usage, but not checked against the official docs. Verify in the next full sweep.
